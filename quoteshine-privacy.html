Quoteshine Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Quoteshine is an app for iPhone that helps you build price quotes and share them as PDF files. It is made by Terror LLC, an Illinois limited liability company ("Terror LLC", "we"). This policy explains what Quoteshine does with your information. The short version: Quoteshine keeps everything on your phone, and we receive none of it.

What we collect

Nothing. Quoteshine has no account, no sign-in, no server and no analytics. It contains no advertising, no crash reporting, no tracking and no third-party code. Terror LLC and any other company receive no information from the app, and the app does not track you. This matches the "Data Not Collected" answer in the App Store privacy label and the privacy manifest inside the app.

What the app stores on your phone

You enter this information, and Quoteshine saves it on your phone so you can use it again:

  • Customer details on each quote: the customer's name, phone number and vehicle, plus the quote's items, prices, notes, dates and total.
  • Your price list: your services and add-ons and their prices.
  • Your business profile: your business name, phone number, email address, logo and quote footer text.
  • A small setting that remembers that you have seen the welcome tour.

This information is kept in the app's own storage area on your phone, which iOS protects with file encryption (the files are protected until you first unlock the phone after it restarts). Other apps cannot read it, and Quoteshine does not send it anywhere.

Your logo

When you add a logo, Quoteshine opens the iOS photo picker. The picker is run by iOS, so Quoteshine never asks for access to your photo library and never sees your other photos. The app receives only the one image you choose. It saves a reduced-size copy (at most 512 pixels wide or tall) that has been re-created without location data or other hidden details from the original photo.

Sharing a quote

A quote PDF leaves your phone only when you tap Share and choose a destination in the iOS share sheet, such as Messages, Mail or Files. The PDF contains the customer's name, phone number and vehicle, the quote items and total, and your business details. The PDF's document properties also hold the customer's name (as the title) and your business name (as the author). Once you send a PDF, the app or service you chose and the person who receives it control their copy under their own policies. Quoteshine cannot see, track or take back a PDF after you share it. While you preview or share a quote, Quoteshine makes a temporary copy of the PDF in the app's protected storage and deletes these temporary copies the next time the app launches.

Links that open in your browser

The Support, Privacy policy and Terms of use rows in Settings open web pages in Safari. These pages are published on the website takagoto.github.io, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. Quoteshine sends those pages nothing about you or your quotes. When you open a page, your browser connects to GitHub, which may log your visit (for example your IP address) under its own privacy statement.

How long information is kept and how to delete it

  • When you delete a quote or a service, it moves to Recently Deleted (Settings, then Recently Deleted). You can restore it there for 30 days. After 30 days Quoteshine removes it permanently.
  • Removing a line from a quote you are still editing happens right away.
  • To delete everything, delete Quoteshine from your phone. That removes all the information the app stored. Terror LLC holds no copy, so we cannot recover or delete anything on your behalf.
  • In the rare case that the app cannot open its saved data, it offers "Start fresh". Starting fresh opens an empty app and sets the old data aside in a protected file inside the app. The app deletes that file automatically after 30 days, and deleting the app removes it sooner. We only ever receive that file if you choose to send it to us when you ask for help.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup for your phone, the quotes, price list and business profile stored by Quoteshine are included in that backup, under your Apple Account and Apple's terms. We never receive them. Deleting the app does not remove copies already in an existing backup; those follow your backup settings with Apple.

Your responsibilities

You decide which customer details to store. You are responsible for having the right to keep and share those details, and for following any laws that apply to you and your customers.

Children

Quoteshine is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Quoteshine changes how it handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If the app ever collected data, this policy and the App Store privacy label would be updated first.
