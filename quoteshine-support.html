Quoteshine Support

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Quoteshine builds itemized car-detailing quotes on your iPhone and shares them as PDFs. It needs no account and works without a signal. If you need help, write to support@terrorapps.com. We usually reply within three business days.

Building and sharing a quote

  1. Open the Quotes tab and tap New quote.
  2. Enter the customer's name (required), phone, and the vehicle (year, make and model), then choose the vehicle size: Compact, Sedan, SUV or Truck.
  3. Tap Add service or add-on and pick items from your price list. The Custom tab adds a one-off charge or a discount. Changing the vehicle size reprices items that came from your price list.
  4. Tap the eye icon to preview the PDF. Tap Share PDF to open the iOS share sheet and choose Messages, Mail or any other app. Sharing is available once the quote has a customer name and at least one item with a total above zero.
  5. A shared quote is marked Sent. Tap it to preview, or choose Edit quote to change it and share again. Swipe a row to duplicate or delete it.

Set your prices once under the Services tab, and add your business name, phone, email, logo and quote footer under Settings, then Business profile. Every PDF says "Estimate only. Not a contract." even if you edit the footer. A quote is an estimate, not a contract, and Quoteshine does not give legal, tax or insurance advice.

Restoring something you deleted

Deleting a quote or a service moves it to Settings, then Recently Deleted. Open it there and tap Restore, and it returns to its list. Items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. After that they are removed permanently and cannot be restored, by you or by us.

Saving a copy and moving to a new phone

Quoteshine 1.0 has no export, no cloud sync and no account. Your quotes, price list and business profile live on your phone. The safety net is your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), which includes the app's data when you restore your phone from it. To keep a copy of an individual quote, share its PDF to Files or Mail.

Deleting your data

  • To delete one quote or service, delete it from its list. It moves to Settings, then Recently Deleted, and is removed permanently after 30 days. There is no earlier purge in version 1.0; to erase everything at once, delete the app.
  • To delete everything, delete Quoteshine from your phone. This removes all the data the app stored on the phone. Terror LLC holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Copies in an existing iPhone backup follow your backup settings in iOS.
  • If the app ever shows "Start fresh" because it cannot open its saved data, the old data is kept in a protected file inside the app for 30 days and then deleted automatically.

Subscriptions and purchases

Quoteshine is free and has no subscription or purchase, so there is nothing to cancel.

Replaying the welcome tour

Open Settings and tap Show welcome tour again.

Reporting a problem

Email support@terrorapps.com and include:

  • what you were doing and what you expected to happen,
  • your iPhone model and iOS version,
  • the Quoteshine version, shown at the bottom of Settings.

Please do not send customer names, phone numbers or other private details. A description or a screenshot with the personal details covered is enough.

Privacy and terms

Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use on this website. Quoteshine collects no data and sends nothing to us.
